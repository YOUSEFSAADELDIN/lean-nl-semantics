Checking natural-language entailment with a proof assistant, not a language model.

Pick a fact below, then a topic. The diagram builds itself one premise at a time, and the reasoner's verdict follows at the end. Everything shown is checked by Lean 4, applying a short, fixed list of logical principles; nothing here is guessed by a language model.

3. Coverage

The system above is deliberately narrow: its scope is the monotonicity and cardinality behavior of a handful of quantifiers over two predicates at a time, not anaphora, tense, comparatives, or the many other phenomena a broader benchmark tests.
