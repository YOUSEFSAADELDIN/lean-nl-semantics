Semanticsrestrictor / scope split

This project treats a determiner as a relation between two predicates over one domain: the restrictor (who we're talking about) and the scope (what's claimed of them), following Barwise & Cooper's generalized-quantifier format, the same shape LeanNLSemantics/FraCaS/Section1/GQ.lean defines Every/Some/AtLeast/AtMost in.
